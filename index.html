How to play

  • ↑Climb to the roof. Arrow keys / WASD, or drag anywhere on a touch screen and hold a direction. One thumb is enough.
  • ■Stay still to brace. After a moment of holding on you lock in: hits hurt half as much and wind can't move you. Grip also recovers while you rest.
  • !Shutters. An amber lamp means a window is about to slam shut. Get off it — a closed shutter crushes your grip, and closed windows block your path.
  • ▼Falling junk. Tenants toss things out. Sidestep, or brace for it. Red arrows mean something heavy is coming down that column.
  • ◀Drones, beams, sparks, wind. Each one warns you first: chevrons for patrol drones, a thin line for orbital beams, flicker for live signs, streaks for gusts.
  • ≈The acid tide rises from the street. Don't dawdle.
  • ◆Pickups. Chips score points, batteries refill grip, hex shields absorb one hit, hearts add a life.
  • ⚑Balconies every 12 floors are checkpoints. Lose your grip and you restart from the last one. Climb without stopping to build FLOW for up to ×5 score.
